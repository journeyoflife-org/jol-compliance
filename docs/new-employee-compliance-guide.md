## Welcome to Journey Of Life!

As a new team member, you play a vital role in protecting the personal data of ~400,000 religious institutions across the EU. This guide covers your compliance obligations.

---

## Checklist: First 30 Days

### Week 1: Foundations

- [ ] **Sign NDA and Acceptable Use Policy** (`security-policies/acceptable-use-policy.md`)
- [ ] **Complete security awareness training** (LMS — mandatory within 30 days)
- [ ] **Review ISMS Policy** (`iso27001/policies/information-security-policy.md`)
- [ ] **Set up MFA** on all Company accounts (SSO, GitHub, VPN)
- [ ] **Receive and configure Company device** (full-disk encryption, endpoint protection)
- [ ] **Review Data Classification Policy** (`security-policies/data-classification-policy.md`)

### Week 2: Role-Specific

- [ ] **Complete GDPR training** (data protection principles, rights, breach reporting)
- [ ] **Review Access Control Procedure** (`iso27001/procedures/access-control-procedure.md`)
- [ ] **Understand your role's access rights** and data handling responsibilities
- [ ] **Review Privacy Policy** relevant to your responsibilities
- [ ] **Read Incident Response Policy** (`security-policies/incident-response-policy.md`) — know how to report incidents

### Week 3–4: Deep Dive

- [ ] **Review Password Policy** (`security-policies/password-policy.md`)
- [ ] **If remote worker:** Review Remote Work Security Policy
- [ ] **If developer:** Review secure coding standards, CI/CD security, and change management procedures
- [ ] **If handling vendor relationships:** Review Vendor Register and DPA template
- [ ] **If DPO/privacy role:** Review ROPA, DSR procedures, and country-specific requirements

## Quick Reference: Incident Reporting

If you suspect a security incident:
1. **Do NOT attempt to investigate yourself**
2. Report immediately to **security@jol-hub.com**
3. For personal data breaches: also notify the DPO
4. Document what you observed (time, systems, actions)

## Annual Obligations

- Annual security awareness training refresh
- Annual policy acknowledgment (Acceptable Use, NDA)
- Participate in quarterly access reviews (confirm or revoke your access)

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of all Journey Of Life information assets by employees, contractors, and third parties. It supports ISO 27001 A.5.10 and SOC 2 CC5.3.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all personnel with access to Company information systems, including: company-issued devices, network resources, email, collaboration tools, code repositories, cloud services, and tenant data.

## 3. General Rules

### 3.1 Permitted Use

- Business purposes aligned with your role and responsibilities
- Limited personal use that does not interfere with work, consume excessive resources, or violate any law

### 3.2 Prohibited Use

- Accessing data or systems beyond your role's authorised scope
- Sharing credentials (passwords, tokens, keys) with any person
- Using Company assets for illegal, fraudulent, or unauthorised commercial activities
- Installing unapproved software on Company devices or production systems
- Disabling, circumventing, or interfering with security controls
- Exfiltrating Company or tenant data to personal accounts or unauthorised storage
- Using Company email or systems to send spam, malware, or harassment

### 3.3 Data Handling

| Classification | Permitted Actions | Restrictions |
|---------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Public** | Share, publish, distribute | Must be approved for public release |
| **Internal** | Use for business, share internally | Do not share externally without approval |
| **Confidential** | Access on need-to-know basis | Encrypted transfer; do not store on personal devices |
| **Restricted** | Access by named individuals only | Field-level encryption; no local storage; no email |

## 4. Device and Endpoint Use

- Full-disk encryption mandatory on all Company devices
- Screen lock: auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity (15 minutes maximum)
- Company devices must run approved endpoint protection
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 1 hour
- Personal devices (BYOD) may only access Company systems via approved secure channels

## 5. Internet and Email

- Do not open suspicious email attachments or links — report to IT
- Do not use Company email for personal account registration
- Web browsing must comply with Company web filtering policies
- Do not use unapproved cloud storage services for Company data

## 6. Clean Desk and Clear Screen

- Sensitive documents (Confidential/Restricted) must be locked away when unattended
- Screens must be locked when leaving the workstation
- Whiteboards with sensitive information must be erased after use
- Printers: collect documents promptly; use secure print for Confidential data

## 7. Social Media and Communications

- Do not disclose Confidential or Restricted information on social media
- Represent the Company professionally in all public communications
- Do not make commitments about security posture or compliance without CISO approval

## 8. Reporting Violations

All personnel must report suspected policy violations to:
- **Email:** security@jol-hub.com
- **Anonymously:** [Whistleblower channel]

Retaliation for good-faith reporting is prohibited.

## 9. Enforcement

Violations may result in:
1. Verbal warning (first minor violation)
2. Written warning (repeated violations)
3. Suspension of access (serious violations)
4. Disciplinary action up to and including termination (gross violations)
5. Legal action where criminal activity is suspected

## 1. Audit Objectives

1. Determine whether the ISMS conforms to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements
2. Determine whether the ISMS is effectively implemented and maintained
3. Identify opportunities for improvement
4. Verify corrective actions from previous audits

## 2. Audit Criteria

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Clauses 4–10 and Annex A
- ISMS policies and procedures (as documented in compliance repository)
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements (GDPR, national laws)
- Contractual requirements (tenant DPAs, SLAs)

## 3. Audit Methodology

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Document review | Policies, procedures, records, evidence |
| Interviews | Personnel responsible for controls |
| Observation | Direct observation of processes and controls |
| Technical testing | Sampling of configurations, access controls, logs |
| Walkthrough | End-to-end process walkthroughs |

## 1. National Data Protection Legislation

| Law | Full Name | Key Provisions |
|-----|----------|---------------|
| **Personas datu apstrādes likums** | Personal Data Processing Law | Supplements GDPR |
| **GDPR** | Directly applicable EU Regulation 2016/679 | Primary framework |
| **ePrivacy** | Electronic Communications Law (Elektronisko sakaru likums) | Cookie consent |

## 2. Data Retention Requirements

| Data Category | Legal Basis | Retention Period |
|--------------|------------|-----------------|
| Financial/tax records | Grāmatvedības likums (Art. 9) | **7 years** |
| Employee records | Darba likums (Labour Law) | **3 years** after termination |
| Accounting documents | Grāmatvedības likums | **7 years** |
| Communication data | Electronic Communications Law | **18 months** |

## 3. Age of Consent: **13 years**

## 4. Breach Notification

- DVI within **72 hours** (GDPR Art. 33)
- Data subjects: without undue delay if high risk (GDPR Art. 34)

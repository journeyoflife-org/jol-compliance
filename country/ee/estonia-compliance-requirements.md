## 1. National Data Protection Legislation

| Law | Full Name | Key Provisions |
|-----|----------|---------------|
| **IKÜM** | Isikuandmete kaitse seadus (Personal Data Protection Act) | Supplements GDPR |
| **GDPR** | Directly applicable EU Regulation 2016/679 | Primary framework |
| **ePrivacy** | Elektroonilise side seadus (Electronic Communications Act) | Cookie consent |

## 2. Data Retention Requirements

| Data Category | Legal Basis | Retention Period |
|--------------|------------|-----------------|
| Financial/tax records | Raamatupidamise seadus §12 | **7 years** |
| Employee records | Töölepingu seadus (Employment Contracts Act) | **3 years** after termination |
| Accounting documents | Raamatupidamise seadus | **7 years** |
| Communication data | Elektroonilise side seadus | **12 months** |

## 3. Age of Consent: **13 years**

## 4. E-Residency Considerations

- Estonian e-residents have equal data protection rights under GDPR
- E-residency Digi-ID processing is subject to GDPR Art. 6 lawful basis
- Platform must support e-residency authentication methods where applicable

## 5. Breach Notification

- AKI within **72 hours** (GDPR Art. 33)
- Data subjects: without undue delay if high risk (GDPR Art. 34)

## 1. National Data Protection Legislation

| Law | Full Name | Key Provisions |
|-----|----------|---------------|
| **ADTAĮ** | Asmens duomenų teisinės apsaugos įstatymas (Law on Legal Protection of Personal Data) | Supplements GDPR with national specifics |
| **GDPR** | Directly applicable EU Regulation 2016/679 | Primary legal framework |
| **ePrivacy** | Implemented via Electronic Communications Act | Cookie consent, electronic marketing |

## 2. Data Retention Requirements

| Data Category | Legal Basis | Retention Period |
|--------------|------------|-----------------|
| Financial/tax records | Mokesčių administravimo įstatymas (Art. 40) | **10 years** |
| Employee records | Darbo kodeksas (Labour Code) | **5 years** after termination |
| Accounting documents | Buhalterinės apskaitos įstatymas (Art. 19) | **10 years** |
| Communication data | Electronic Communications Act | **24 months** |
| System logs | Internal policy (based on legitimate interest) | **12 months** |

## 3. Age of Consent for Information Society Services

- Lithuania: **14 years** (GDPR Art. 8 allows member states to set between 13–16)
- Below 14: Parental/guardian consent required

## 4. DPO Requirements

- DPO mandatory when core activities involve processing special category data on large scale (GDPR Art. 37(1)(c))
- DPO contact: duomenu.apsauga@jol-hub.com
- DPO must be registered with VDAI

## 5. Breach Notification

| Requirement | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Supervisory authority notification | VDAI within **72 hours** (GDPR Art. 33) |
| Notification form | Available on VDAI website |
| Data subject notification | Without undue delay if high risk (GDPR Art. 34) |
| Processor to controller notification | **24 hours** |

## 6. Special Category Data (Art. 9)

- Religious affiliation: Explicit consent required or substantial public interest basis
- Health data: Explicit consent or healthcare provision basis
- Children's data: Parental consent below age 14

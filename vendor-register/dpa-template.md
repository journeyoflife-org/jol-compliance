## DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between:

**Controller:** [Tenant Institution Name], [Address], [Registration Number]  
**Processor:** Journey Of Life UAB, [Address], [Registration Number]

### 1. Subject Matter and Duration

1.1 The Processor provides a multi-tenant SaaS platform for religious institutions ("Platform").  
1.2 This DPA governs the processing of personal data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller.  
1.3 Duration: For the term of the main service agreement between the parties.

### 2. Nature and Purpose of Processing

2.1 Processing activities: collection, storage, organisation, structuring, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, alignment, restriction, erasure, and destruction.  
2.2 Purpose: Delivery of the Platform services as described in the service agreement.

### 3. Categories of Data Subjects and Personal Data

| Data Subject Category | Personal Data Categories |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| Institution members | Name, email, date of birth, contact details |
| Clergy / staff | Name, role, contact details, employment information |
| Children (under 16) | Name, date of birth, parental consent records |
| Special category (Art. 9) | Religious affiliation, health data (if applicable) |

### 4. Obligations of the Processor (Art. 28(3))

The Processor shall:
- (a) Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller
- (b) Ensure personnel are committed to confidentiality
- (c) Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (Art. 32)
- (d) Not engage sub-processors without prior written consent (Art. 28(2))
- (e) Assist the Controller with data subject rights requests (Art. 15–22)
- (f) Assist with DPIA and prior consultation (Art. 35–36)
- (g) Delete or return all personal data at end of processing (Art. 28(3)(g))
- (h) Make available all information necessary for audits (Art. 28(3)(h))

### 5. Sub-Processing (Art. 28(2) and (4))

5.1 The Controller grants general authorisation for the sub-processors listed in the Vendor Register.  
5.2 The Processor shall notify the Controller of any intended changes ≥30 days in advance.  
5.3 The Controller may object to a new sub-processor within the notification period.  
5.4 Sub-processors are bound by equivalent data protection obligations.

### 6. International Transfers (Chapter V)

6.1 Personal data shall not be transferred outside the EU/EEA without appropriate safeguards.  
6.2 Where transfers occur: Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or adequacy decision.  
6.3 Transfer Impact Assessments (TIA) conducted for all non-EU transfers.

### 7. Security Measures (Art. 32)

The Processor implements:
- Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Multi-tenant data isolation
- Access controls with MFA and RBAC
- Vulnerability scanning and penetration testing
- Incident response with ≤24-hour notification to Controller
- Regular backups with tested recovery procedures

### 8. Breach Notification (Art. 33)

8.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller of a personal data breach within **24 hours** of becoming aware.  
8.2 Notification includes: nature of breach, categories affected, likely consequences, measures taken.  
8.3 The Processor shall assist the Controller in breach notification to supervisory authority and data subjects.

### 9. Audit Rights (Art. 28(3)(h))

9.1 The Controller may audit the Processor's compliance annually upon reasonable notice.  
9.2 The Processor shall provide SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 audit reports upon request.  
9.3 The Processor shall cooperate with audits by supervisory authorities.

### 10. Data Return and Deletion

10.1 Upon termination, the Processor shall return or delete all personal data within 30 days.  
10.2 Deletion confirmation provided in writing within 10 business days.  
10.3 Legal retention obligations may require extended storage of specific data categories.

### 11. Liability and Indemnification

Each party's liability is governed by the main service agreement and applicable law.

### 12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This DPA is governed by the laws of the Republic of Lithuania. Disputes resolved by Vilnius courts.
